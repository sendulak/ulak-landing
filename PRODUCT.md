# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Startup founders, marketing teams at SMBs, and solo creators (newsletter writers, course creators, indie hackers). They land here mid-research: evaluating whether ulak can replace their current email tool's one-size-fits-all blasts. They're skeptical of "AI-powered" claims but hungry for something that actually works. They've sent enough campaigns to know the open rates are bad and the copy feels hollow.

## Product Purpose

ulak writes a different marketing email for every person on your list. One campaign prompt in, thousands of individually written emails out. It exists because bulk email is broken: everyone gets the same message, and everyone ignores it. Success looks like a visitor understanding the product in under 10 seconds and signing up to try it.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, direct, confident. No fluff, no hand-holding, no buzzwords. Speaks like a smart colleague who cuts to the point. Respects the visitor's time and intelligence.

## Anti-references

- **Mailchimp**: Too playful, too quirky, too many illustrations. ulak is a tool, not a toy.
- **Generic SaaS templates**: Gradient heroes, stock photos, buzzword-heavy copy, identical feature card grids. The opposite of distinctive.
- **Enterprise email tools** (Salesforce Marketing Cloud, Adobe Campaign): Dense, corporate, feature-overloaded. ulak is simple by design.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the difference**: The product's value is visible. Don't describe personalization; demonstrate it side by side.
2. **Respect the scroll**: Every section earns its place. If it doesn't add new information, cut it.
3. **Confidence over decoration**: Let the product speak. Strong typography and clear hierarchy over ornamental design.
4. **Speed is trust**: The page should feel fast to load and fast to read. No friction between landing and understanding.
5. **One idea per fold**: Each viewport carries one dominant concept. Don't compete for attention within a section.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Reduced motion support already in place; maintain it. Ensure all interactive elements have visible focus states. Body text contrast must hit 4.5:1 against all backgrounds.
